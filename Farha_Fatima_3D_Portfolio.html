About

I'm Farha Fatima, a digital marketer based in Ramdevguda, Ibrahimbagh, Hyderabad. With a B.Com degree and hands-on experience, I help small businesses show up on Google and look professional — both online and offline.

I manage Google Business Profiles, improve local SEO, run Google Ads campaigns, and track performance through Google Search Console and basic GA4 reporting. I also design creatives in Canva — social posts, flyers, and simple brand kits — for a consistent, professional look.

My time working with an ads agency on flex posters, banners, standees and print creatives means I can also coordinate offline branding — understanding requirements, briefing designs, and managing print timelines.

I bring practical digital skills together with real coordination experience, so small Hyderabad businesses can build trust both on Google and on the street.
